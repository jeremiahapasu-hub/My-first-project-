Load a signal export to analyse it

Everything runs inside this page. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and closing the tab discards it. Drop a CSV, JSON or plain-text export below — column names are matched loosely, so most formats work as-is.

This is the analysis half of the full application, running without a server. It reads history and computes statistics; it does not connect to any broker and cannot place trades.

Not financial advice. This analyses historical records you provide. It is not a recommendation to trade, not a forecast, and past patterns have no obligation to continue. Binary options carry a substantial risk of losing your capital.

The sample data is synthetic — generated by a seeded random number generator to exercise the charts. It is not market history.

Full version (accounts, saved history, Excel/PDF export) runs as a Next.js app with PostgreSQL.
